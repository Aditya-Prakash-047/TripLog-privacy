TripLog

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

TripLog ("TripLog", "we", "us", or "our") is a free mobile application that allows users to record journeys, track routes using device location, visualize travel paths on maps, and search for locations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TripLog may access or process, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Location Information

Location information is an important part of TripLog's core functionality.

When you use TripLog's journey-recording functionality, the application may access your device's location to determine your position and record the route you travel.

Depending on the device and permissions granted, this may include precise geographic coordinates.

Location information may be used to:

  • record a journey;
  • construct and display a route or path;
  • show your position on a map;
  • provide location-related functionality;
  • support location and place searches.

TripLog does not use location information for advertising.

2. User Control

TripLog requests location permission through Android's permission system.

You can choose whether to grant location access and can revoke that access at any time through your Android device settings.

If location permission is denied or revoked, features that require location information, including journey recording, may not work.

3. Route and Journey Information

When you record a journey, TripLog may process geographic location points used to construct the recorded route.

A recorded route may reveal information about places you visited and the path you travelled. You should therefore treat recorded journeys as potentially sensitive personal information.

4. Map and Location Services

TripLog uses map and location-related services to provide mapping, geocoding, place search, and related functionality.

The current application may use OpenStreetMap-based map data and LocationIQ services for map and location functionality.

When a feature requires communication with an external map or location service, information necessary to provide that feature may be transmitted to the relevant service.

Depending on the service and request, this may include search queries, geographic coordinates, IP address, network information, or other technical information required to process the request.

These third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Personal Information

The current version of TripLog does not require users to create an account to use its core functionality.

TripLog does not intentionally require users to provide their name, phone number, email address, contacts, or financial information to use the core journey-recording functionality.

6. Advertising

The current version of TripLog does not intentionally display advertising and does not use location information for advertising.

If advertising or advertising-related services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

7. Data Sharing

TripLog does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide map, location, geocoding, or search functionality.

TripLog does not share location information with third parties for advertising purposes.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by TripLog.

However, no method of electronic transmission, communication, or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

TripLog retains information only for as long as necessary to provide the functionality for which it is processed, subject to how the current version of the application stores and manages recorded journeys.

If future versions introduce server-side accounts or cloud storage, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those practices.

10. Children's Privacy

TripLog is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the application's functionality.

11. Third-Party Services

TripLog may rely on third-party services for map, geographic, geocoding, and location functionality.

These providers may process technical or location-related information required to provide their services.

Users should also review the privacy policies of applicable third-party services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TripLog's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be made available through this same Privacy Policy URL.
